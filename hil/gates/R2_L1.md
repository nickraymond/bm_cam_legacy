**Question:** with the first-send hold at uptime 230 s, does a 40-chunk heal riding the wake (a) make the hold get
skipped by the budget check, or (b) add first-send loss to the new image?

## Forced heals (bench knob, branch `hil/r2-l1-forced-heals` f393fb3, stacked on the R2-DELAY patch 80c3662)

`<app>/r2_force_skip_n = 40` (absent file / 0 = no-op): when the wake's START goes out, its key + length M arm a skip
of 40 consecutive chunk indices starting at M // 3; those chunk messages are not written to the UART (their pacing slot
is still spent), logged once as `[R2FORCE] key=… M=… skipping 40 chunk(s): a-b`. Heal chunks (other keys, sent before
START) are never touched. The backend then sees exactly 40 missing → 1 rsd per wake (cap 40) → it reaches the unit
~2 wakes later (SPOT-33507C: a command sent after :10 lands at the next :10 sync, applied at the following boot), so
from the 3rd wake on every wake carries one 40-chunk heal of the image 2 wakes back.
Tested on the desk: 195-chunk START → 40 dropped (65–104), other-key chunks pass, summary JSON-safe; 202 stills /
heal tests pass.

## Measures per wake

`[R2DELAY]` line: heal_msgs, budget_left, wait, burst_est, skipped (= hold applied?); `[HEAL] sent N heal chunk(s)`
+ the rsd that asked for them (requested vs served, from the console + cycle log); the NEW image's first-send loss
**on the non-forced indices** (hil_r2_score.py, forced range excluded — the forced 40 are not loss); contiguous gaps;
queue_full vs the Spotter's HDR/health/report; burst end; wake→halt; the healed image's backend completion.
Arithmetic (EM): ~570 s − ~232 s hold ≈ 338 s left vs 195 + 40 msgs × 1.0 s ≈ 240 s → expected to fit, margin ~100 s
(the budget is measured from cycle start, not boot; the logged budget_left decides).

## Rules (pre-registered — approved by the EM 2026-10-07)

- n = 6 wakes that carry a 40-chunk heal.
- **PASS:** hold applied on 6/6 heal wakes AND median L1 first-send loss (non-forced indices) ≤ median R2-DELAY
  baseline B-arm loss (same metric) **+ 1 percentage point** (~2 chunks; a 0 % baseline would otherwise fail on one
  stray chunk).
- **STOP (answered):** 2 budget skips on heal wakes.
- Safety stop as R2-DELAY: burst ends after :09:00 or wake→halt > 570 s.
