## 3. Test cards (handbook §3), pre-registered

### T-DRY: bench dry run, bmcam004 only
- **Question:** does pool mode run the whole sequence unattended and leave production untouched?
- **Conditions:** bmcam004 on the bench, bus held on, nereus000 console; compressed timings: calibration 2 min @ 10 s,
  loop 3 iterations @ 2 min, 1 preview, 1 mark, then stop, pool mode off.
- **n:** 1 full sequence (a tooling check, not a statistical claim); then T-LAND on both units adds 2 more.
- **Pass (all):** 12 ± 1 calibration DNG+JPEG pairs; 3 × (pjpg + nrjxl + mp4) with plausible sizes (nrjxl ~40–80 KB,
  mp4 ~5–8 MB); every command answered `OK id=` on the console; preview rebuilt byte-identical on nereus000 ≤ 30 s after
  the command; per-frame calibration time ≤ 10 s; loop iteration time ≤ 5 min (half the 10-min budget); peak RSS < 150 MB;
  Pi never halted; after `pool_mode off` the crontab and camera_config.yaml hash are identical to before.
- **Stop:** any FAIL → fix and re-run (it's cheap); a hard cut or a Spotter reset → stop and log.
- **Cost:** ~45 min of bmcam004 + SPOT-31593C.

### T-LAND: land rehearsal, both units, Nick moves the board
- **Question:** will Tuesday's calibration frames give usable intrinsics, and does everything work on two units at once?
- **Conditions:** both units, real 20 min calibration @ 10 s with Nick moving the ChArUco board in air; then preview of the v3
  card at 0.75 m and 1.5 m (in-air stand-ins for 1 m / 2 m behind the flat port); 2 loop iterations; parallel download.
- **n:** 2 units × 1 sequence.
- **Pass:** board detected in ≥ 15 distinct poses per unit; calibration RMS reprojection error < 1 px (in-air intrinsics,
  engineering check only); v3 card patches sharp at both distances at focus 1.82 (visual + Laplacian variance, Nick's call);
  download rate measured → projected full-pool pull (both units) ≤ 1 h; Spotter battery drop over the session recorded.
- **Stop:** board detection < 10 poses → stop, fix capture settings (exposure/blur) before anything else.
- **Cost:** ~1.5 h both units + Nick 20 min.

### T-RESTORE: back to production
- **Question:** does a unit return to exactly the RC2 production state after pool mode?
- **Pass:** crontab + config hash identical to the pre-pool snapshot (+ focus manual 1.82); next hourly wake delivers a JPEG XL
