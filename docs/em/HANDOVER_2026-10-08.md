  - An **inbound command adds 36–46 s** to that sync.
  - **Notecard hand-off stalls** with no visible trigger at ~:01–:03.
  - Loss outside the holds is 0.1–1 %.
- **Report minute** = post-boot GPS time rounded up to the next 5-min mark. Every Spotter reset re-rolls it. **No customer setting pins it** (Sofar).
